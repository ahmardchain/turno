# TURNO — Brand

**Name:** Turno
**Tagline:** Everyone gets their turn.
**One-liner:** Savings circles where the contract holds the pot — and nobody can run with it.

## Why this name
"Turno" = *turn* (Spanish/Italian, understood globally). The whole product is about turns:
whose turn it is to pay in, whose turn it is to collect. Four promises in five letters:
short, pronounceable in Lagos, London and São Paulo, and it is *not* another
Pay/Split/Escrow compound name.

Checked 2026-10-22 before adopting: no savings-circle, escrow or payments product
named Turno. The crowded names in this space — Ajo, Alajo, Kolo, Susu, Osusu, Solva,
Truzo — were all rejected for direct collisions.

## The wedge (why Turno is not the existing ajo apps)
Every existing ajo/esusu app found is either a ledger or a custodian:
- Turnwyse: "tracking only — no wallet, no escrow, nothing for anyone to abscond with"
  (their words) — the money still moves hand-to-hand off-app.
- AjoMoney / AjoLedger / Osusu MoneyFlow: contribution tracking, reminders, reports.
- CircleFunds: holds funds in *licensed custodial escrow*, in naira, Nigeria-only.

Turno is none of those. The **smart contract** holds the USDC on Arc. Not the
organiser, not Turno, not a company. The payout fires automatically inside the same
transaction as the final contribution for a round. There is no account to abscond
with, no admin who can delay a payout, and no custodian to trust or license.

## Voice
Plain, warm, zero crypto jargon in user-facing copy. Words we use: circle, turn,
pot, pay in, collect. Words we never use: ROSCA (only in judge-facing docs),
yield, protocol, onchain (in app copy).

## Look
- Ink: `#0B0F0C` (near-black green)
- Turn lime: `#C8F169` (primary accent — "your turn" highlights)
- Paper: `#F7F5EF` (backgrounds)
- Type: heavy grotesk for headlines, tabular numbers for amounts.
- Motif: a ring of dots, one filled — the circle, and whose turn it is.
